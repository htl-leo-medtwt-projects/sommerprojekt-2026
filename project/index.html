Dungeoner

An isometric dungeon crawler

The Story

Kael was a blacksmith in the river town of Veldmoor — not a hero, not a soldier. Just a father with a trade and enough. Then the Dungeon opened.

A sickness called the Grey moved through town. People stopped waking up — breathing, heart beating, but behind the eyes: nothing. His daughter Mira was among them.

The scholars say something ancient at the bottom of the dungeon is draining life from the land. Kill it, and the taken return. Every soldier they sent did not come back.

Kael is not a soldier. He is a blacksmith who knows which metal holds and which fails — and a father who cannot walk past that bed one more morning and do nothing.

He walks in. The gate closes behind him.

How to Play

Objective

Fight through the dungeon and defeat the ancient creature in the depths — the only thing that can allow Mira's return. Collect treasure, grow stronger, and go deeper.

Controls

Move your avatar with WASD.
Left-click to attack; hold right-click to block — blocking halves the damage you take.
Press T on a transfer point to move to the next level. Press E to use a Teleport Scroll and return home instantly.

Combat

Running into an enemy starts a turn-based fight. You and your opponent alternate attacks each second. Damage dealt depends on your attack versus the enemy's defense. If you die, you lose all gold you are currently carrying — your equipment is always kept.

Leaving the Dungeon

Fight through 10 dungeon levels and defeat the final boss, then return home to keep your gold safe. You can also deposit gold into the bank at any time to protect it from death — but you will have to restart the dungeon from the beginning.

Treasure & Equipment

The dungeon contains gold, gems, and treasure chests. Spend your collected gold in the shop to buy weapons and armor, permanently increasing your attack and defense. Teleport Scrolls are also available if you need a quick escape.
